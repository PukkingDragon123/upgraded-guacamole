# Audio credits

Every sound and music track in FISHTOP is a recording or composition from the sources below. Nothing is
under a licence that needs attribution, a non-commercial clause or share-alike: all of it is **CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication)** or **US public domain** (works of the US National Park Service). Packs offered
under several licences (marked "multi") are used under their CC0 option. Credit is given anyway, with thanks.

Clips were trimmed, cut into seamless loops, loudness-normalised and re-encoded as OGG Vorbis (one-shots and
loop beds mono 32 kHz in `assets/sfx/`, music stereo 44.1 kHz ~48 kbps in `assets/music/`). File name = clip id
(see `SAMPLE_MAP` in `src/core/samples.js`). `node tools/gen-samples.mjs` embeds them into the build.
